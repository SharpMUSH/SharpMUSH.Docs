# Sharpie marketing assets

`share-card.html` is the editable source for the 1280 × 640 social card. The mascot illustration is a native-alpha generated asset of its own. Text is composed in HTML rather than baked into generation. The PNG export uses an opaque white background for predictable previews.

Palette authority: mint green `#00DDB3`, ink `#17333E`, white. Keep flat mascot fills, rounded white hind paws and complete silhouettes. Do not use palette normalization. The `.provenance.json` files record generation sources and export checks.

The generated share image is published at `/social/sharpie-docs.png`; Starlight's configured head supplies the absolute production URL, image dimensions and alternate text for Open Graph and Twitter. Per-page titles and descriptions remain Starlight's normal values.

The SharpMUSH M is the primary brand identifier. `sharpmush-logo.svg` is a copy of the repository’s canonical logo used in the share-card source. Sharpie is a supporting mascot, not a replacement for the M.
