The web portal's navigation is the rail down the left of a desktop screen, the menu that slides in on a phone,
and the tab bar along the bottom of a phone. Staff arrange it for everyone at **Admin > Portal > Navigation**
(`/admin/navigation`), which needs the `layout.admin` permission. Players see what staff arranged. They can't
rearrange it, but they can open and close pop-out groups in the phone menu.

There is no game command for this: the navigation only exists in the portal.

## What you can change

- **Groups.** Make a group, name it, and choose how it is shown. Move a group up or down.
- **Order.** Move anything up or down in its group, or to another group. Drag it, use the arrow buttons, or
  pick a group from **Move to**.
- **Hidden.** Take something off the navigation. It still works for whoever may use it, and the
  [command palette](/guides/command-palette) still finds it.
- **Links.** Add a link to a portal page or another website, such as your game's Discord. A link can be
  limited to people with a permission.
- **Phone tabs.** Choose the three tabs between **Home** and **Menu**.

Nothing here changes who can open what. The navigation only shows a page to people who could already open
it, wherever you put it. A group with nothing in it for someone isn't shown to them.

## How a group is shown

Each group has a **Shown as** setting. On a desktop rail:

| Shown as | On the rail | In the phone menu |
|---|---|---|
| **Each item on its own** | A button for each item, under the group's name. | The group's name, then its items. |
| **One button, with a sidebar** | One button that opens the first item. An application's page lists the group's other applications in its sidebar. | The group's name, then its items. |
| **One button that opens a list** | One button. Choosing it opens the group's items beside the rail. | A heading you can open and close. |

Use **Each item on its own** for the few things players use all the time. Use the other two when a group
holds many applications and would make the rail long.

A group shown as a list draws its first four items' icons on its button, unless you give the group an icon
of its own. Type a Material icon name, such as `forum`, in **Icon**.

The list opens when you click its button, not when you point at it. **Escape**, a click elsewhere, or
following a link closes it. Up and Down move between its links.

In the phone menu, a group shown as a list is open while it holds the page you are on. Otherwise it stays
as you last left it in that browser.

## When the rail runs out of room

On a short screen, the groups that don't fit go into **More**, a button at the bottom of the groups that
opens a list of them, each under its name. Make the window taller and they come back.

## New applications

When a package adds a portal application, it asks for a group through its **Nav placement**
([Portal Applications](/guides/applications)). If you have a group with that name, the application joins
it. If you don't, it gets a new group of that name, shown as **One button, with a sidebar**. The editor marks
anything you haven't placed yet as **New**.

To decide for yourself where each new application goes, turn off **Show newly installed applications
straight away**. A new application then waits under **Hidden** until you move it.

Uninstalling an application leaves its place in your layout, marked **Not installed**. If the application
is installed again, it goes back where you had it. Remove the row to forget it.

## Add a link

<Steps>

1. Under the group the link goes in, fill in **Link name** and **Address**. The address is a portal page
   starting with `/`, such as `/wiki/rules`, or a website starting with `https://`.

2. Optionally, type a permission, such as `wiki.admin`, so only people who hold it see the link.

3. Press **Add link**, then **Save**.

</Steps>

A link to another website opens in a new tab. Links can't be hidden: remove one instead.

## Choose the phone tabs

A phone shows a tab bar along the bottom: **Home**, up to three tabs you choose, and **Menu**, which opens
everything. Under **Phone tab bar**, choose each tab. A tab can be one item, or a whole group. A group's tab
opens its items in a sheet that rises from the bottom of the screen.

A tab someone can't open isn't shown to them. Tablets use the phone menu and tab bar too.

## Preview, save and start over

**Preview** shows the navigation as a visitor, a player, or staff who can open everything. Press **Save**
to give it to everyone. A portal someone already has open shows it after they reload the page.

If someone else saved while you were editing, your save is refused and nothing of yours is lost from the
page. Press **Reload** to see their version, then make your changes again.

**Reset to default** removes your groups, order, hidden items and links, and puts back the Play and World
groups every game starts with.

<Aside>
Every save and reset is recorded in the audit log, as `navigation.save` and `navigation.reset`.
</Aside>
