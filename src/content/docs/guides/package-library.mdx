Start with the part of your world you want to build. Each guide explains the player commands and the
staff setup for that system.

## Explore common systems

The [bulletin boards](/guides/bboards/) keep community discussions available after a player logs out.
[Radio](/guides/radio/) carries conversations across the game.
The [Wiki Reader](/guides/wiki-reader/) brings your game's longer writing into a MU\* client,
and the [Line Editor](/guides/line-editor/) lets builders write and check attributes without leaving it.

For installing, upgrading or authoring packages, read [Softcode Packages](/guides/packages/).
The [package format reference](/reference/package-format/) describes their declarations.
