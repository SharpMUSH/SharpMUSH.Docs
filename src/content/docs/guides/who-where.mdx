---
title: Who and Where
description: Seeing who is connected with +who, and where they are with +where, grouped by location or sorted by location or name.
---

The **who-where** package gives a game two commands:

- `+who` lists everyone connected, how long each has been idle and how long they have been on.
- `+where` shows where everyone connected is standing.

In the game, `+help who` and `+help where` have the same commands in short form.

## +where

`+where` has three views. The examples below are what Mira saw with seven others connected.

### By location

`+where` on its own lists each location once, with how many players are there and who they are.
Locations are sorted by name, and so are the players in each.

```
> +where
╔══════════════════════════════════╡ Where ╞═════════════════════════════════╗
║ Location       Players  Who                                                ║
║ -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ║
║ Harbour Watch        1  Bram                                               ║
║ Market Square        3  Ash, Sorrel, Tomas                                 ║
║ Room Zero            1  God                                                ║
║ The Old Mill         2  Elin, Mira                                         ║
║ -                    1  Kit                                                ║
╠════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║ 8 connected                                                                ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

Two rooms with the same name get a row each.

### One player per line

`+where/room` lists one player per line, sorted by location and then by name:

```
> +where/room
╔════════════════════════════╡ Where by location ╞═══════════════════════════╗
║ Name    Location                                                           ║
║ -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ║
║ Bram    Harbour Watch                                                      ║
║ Ash     Market Square                                                      ║
║ Sorrel  Market Square                                                      ║
║ Tomas   Market Square                                                      ║
║ God     Room Zero                                                          ║
║ Elin    The Old Mill                                                       ║
║ Mira    The Old Mill                                                       ║
║ Kit     -                                                                  ║
╠════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║ 8 connected                                                                ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

`+where/name` lists the same, sorted by name:

```
> +where/name
╔══════════════════════════════╡ Where by name ╞═════════════════════════════╗
║ Name    Location                                                           ║
║ -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ║
║ Ash     Market Square                                                      ║
║ Bram    Harbour Watch                                                      ║
║ Elin    The Old Mill                                                       ║
║ God     Room Zero                                                          ║
║ Kit     -                                                                  ║
║ Mira    The Old Mill                                                       ║
║ Sorrel  Market Square                                                      ║
║ Tomas   Market Square                                                      ║
╠════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║ 8 connected                                                                ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

Any other switch is refused:

```
> +where/bogus
[WHERE] Warning: +where has no /bogus switch. +where/room lists by location, +where/name by name.
```

### Unfindable players

A player who is unfindable, or who stands in an unfindable room, is listed at `-` rather than
left out. In the examples, Kit is in Smugglers' Cove, which is set `UNFINDABLE`. This holds for
staff too: `+where` looks players up as its own object, not as the reader.

## +who

`+who` lists everyone connected, with their idle and connected times. A Status column appears
when another package gives some player a status, such as `+lfrp` from
[RP Finder](/guides/rp-finder). How a package adds one is in `+help who filters`.

## Installing

who-where ships with the server but is not installed on a new game. An administrator installs
it from the portal: open **Packages** in the admin area, choose **Browse**, and install
`who-where`. It needs `plus-help`, which a new game already has.

See [Softcode Packages](/guides/packages) for how installing, upgrading and rolling back work.
