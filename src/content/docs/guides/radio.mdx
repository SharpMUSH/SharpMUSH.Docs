**Radio** gives the game a set of **frequencies**. Tune in to one and you hear everything said on
it, wherever you are in the game, and you can speak on it yourself. A frequency keeps what was said,
so you can read back the calls you missed. Each frequency has its own admins, who run it, and
moderators, who keep order, and it can be locked so only some players hear it.

<SharpieIllustration scene="radio" />

| You are | You want to | Read |
|---|---|---|
| A player | find a frequency and tune in | [Tuning in](#tuning-in) |
| A player | speak, pose and read back what was said | [Speaking](#speaking), [Recalling](#recalling) |
| A player | use a callsign, a title or a colour, or go quiet | [Your settings](#your-settings) |
| A moderator | stop someone speaking, or let a player in | [Moderating a frequency](#moderating-a-frequency) |
| A frequency's admin | create, rename, lock or delete a frequency | [Running a frequency](#running-a-frequency) |
| Staff | install it and choose who runs the radio | [Setting up the radio](#setting-up-the-radio) |

In the game, `+help radio` has the same commands in short form, and `+help radio moderators` and
`+help radio admins` the rest.

The examples follow Eli, a player, and Dana, a member of staff, on the frequency Harbour Watch.

## Tuning in

`+radio` on its own lists the frequencies you may tune in to. The **You** column says `on` for one
you are tuned in to, `gagged` for one you have gagged, and `-` for the rest:

```text
> +radio
═══════════════════════════════════╡ Radio ╞══════════════════════════════════
Frequency      Category  You  About
Harbour Watch  Port      -    Calls from the harbour patrol boats.
Tower Ops      Port      -    -
═════════════════════╡ +radio/join <frequency> tunes in ╞═════════════════════
```

`+radio/join <frequency>` tunes in, and `+radio/leave <frequency>` tunes out again. You stay tuned in
when you log off, so you are still on the frequency next time:

```text
> +radio/join Harbour Watch
[RADIO] Done: You tune in to Harbour Watch.
> +radio/leave Harbour
[RADIO] Done: You tune out of Harbour Watch.
```

You only need to type enough of a name to tell it from the others: `Harbour` or `harb` is enough
here. A frequency you may not tune in to is not in your list, and joining it is refused:

```text
> +radio/join Tower Ops
[RADIO] Error: You may not tune in to Tower Ops.
```

`+radio/examine <frequency>` shows one frequency: what it is for, who is listening now, who runs it,
and your own settings on it.

```text
> +radio/examine Harbour Watch
═══════════════════════════════╡ Harbour Watch ╞══════════════════════════════
Category:   Port
About:      Calls from the harbour patrol boats.
You:        tuned in
Title:      none
Alterego:   none
Listening:  Dana and Eli
Admins:     Dana
Moderators: none
Members:    none
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

## Speaking

`+radio <frequency>=<message>` speaks on a frequency you are tuned in to. Start the message with `:`
to pose, or `;` to pose without a space. Everyone tuned in hears it, wherever they are:

```text
> +radio harb=Tower, we have an unlit vessel off the north mole.
<Harbour Watch> Eli says, "Tower, we have an unlit vessel off the north mole."
> +radio harb=:acknowledges. Sending a launch.
<Harbour Watch> Dana acknowledges. Sending a launch.
> +radio harb=;'s launch is already out.
<Harbour Watch> Eli's launch is already out.
```

The name matches only the frequencies you are tuned in to, so speaking on one you have not joined
is refused:

```text
> +radio Tower=Hello?
[RADIO] Warning: You are not tuned in to a frequency called 'Tower'. +radio lists them.
```

## Recalling

`+radio/recall <frequency>` shows the last 50 lines said on a frequency, and
`+radio/recall <frequency>=<lines>` as many as you ask for, up to 500. You can recall any frequency
you may tune in to, without tuning in first.

```text
> +radio/recall Harbour Watch=2
═══════════════════════════╡ Recall: Harbour Watch ╞══════════════════════════
<Harbour Watch> Dana acknowledges. Sending a launch.
<Harbour Watch> Eli's launch is already out.
═══════════════════════════════╡ End of recall ╞══════════════════════════════
```

A frequency keeps 500 lines unless its admin sets another number; see
[What a frequency keeps](#what-a-frequency-keeps).

## Your settings

Each of these is yours alone and applies to one frequency. Leave the part after `=` empty to clear
it: `+radio/title Harbour Watch=` takes the title away.

| Type | To |
|---|---|
| `+radio/title <frequency>=<title>` | put a title before your name there, up to 40 characters |
| `+radio/alterego <frequency>=<name>` | speak there under another name, such as a callsign, up to 30 characters |
| `+radio/color <frequency>=<colour>` | see that frequency's name in a colour of your own |
| `+radio/gag <frequency>` | hear nothing from it until `+radio/ungag <frequency>` or you disconnect |
| `+radio/gag all` | gag every frequency you are tuned in to; `+radio/ungag all` ends it |

A title and an alterego together:

```text
> +radio/title Harbour Watch=Bosun
[RADIO] Done: Your title on Harbour Watch is now Bosun.
> +radio Harbour=Launch two is away.
<Harbour Watch> Bosun Eli says, "Launch two is away."
> +radio/alterego Harbour Watch=Watch Two
[RADIO] Done: You speak on Harbour Watch as Watch Two now.
> +radio Harbour=Launch two is away.
<Harbour Watch> Bosun Watch Two says, "Launch two is away."
```

A line keeps the alterego it was said under, so the recall still shows `Watch Two` after you clear
it. The title is shown as it is when the line is read, so clearing it takes it off the old lines in
the recall too.

A colour is a theme colour such as `info`, `primary` or `success`, which follows your own
[`@theme`](/guides/layout-themes/), or `ansi()` codes such as `hg`. Without one, a frequency's name
is in the `info` colour.

A gag lasts until you ungag or disconnect, and while it lasts you are left out of the **Listening**
list. You are still tuned in, and can still speak and recall:

```text
> +radio/gag Harbour Watch
[RADIO] Done: You hear nothing from Harbour Watch until you ungag it or disconnect.
> +radio/ungag Harbour Watch
[RADIO] Done: You hear Harbour Watch again.
```

## Moderating a frequency

A frequency's moderators keep order on it. Its admins are moderators too, and so are staff with the
wizard role or the Radio Admin role, on every frequency.

`+radio/restrict <frequency>=<player>/<duration>` stops a player speaking there, for a time such as
`30m`, `2h` or `7d`. Without a duration it lasts until a moderator lifts it with
`+radio/unrestrict <frequency>=<player>`. A restricted player can still listen and recall, and is
told when it starts and when it is lifted:

```text
> +radio/restrict Harbour Watch=Eli/30m
[RADIO] Done: Eli may not speak on Harbour Watch until Fri Oct 16 01:22:53 2026.
```

Eli hears:

```text
[RADIO] You may not speak on Harbour Watch until Fri Oct 16 01:22:53 2026.
> +radio Harbour=Testing.
[RADIO] Error: You may not speak on Harbour Watch.
```

```text
> +radio/unrestrict Harbour Watch=Eli
[RADIO] Done: Eli may speak on Harbour Watch again.
```

`+radio/memadd <frequency>=<player>` puts a player on the frequency's **members** list, and
`+radio/memrem` takes them off. A member can always tune in and speak, whatever the frequency's lock
says, which is how a closed frequency lets its people in (see [Locks](#locks)):

```text
> +radio/memadd Tower Ops=Eli
[RADIO] Done: Eli is one of the members of Tower Ops now.
```

Anyone who is not a moderator is refused with `Only a moderator of <frequency> can do that.`

## Running a frequency

A frequency's admins run it. Whoever creates a frequency is its first admin, and staff with the
wizard role or the Radio Admin role are admins of every frequency. Only they may create one; anyone
else is refused with `Only a radio admin can create frequencies.`

`+radio/create <name>=<category>` creates a frequency. The category is optional and only groups the
list. A name is up to 30 letters, digits, spaces, dots and dashes.

```text
> +radio/create Harbour Watch=Port
[RADIO] Done: Created Harbour Watch. +radio/join Harbour Watch tunes in.
> +radio/desc Harbour Watch=Calls from the harbour patrol boats.
[RADIO] Done: Set the description of Harbour Watch.
```

`/rename` and `/category` change how it is listed. Renaming keeps everything said on it, and
everyone stays tuned in:

```text
> +radio/category Tower Ops=Air
[RADIO] Done: Tower Ops is in Air now.
> +radio/rename Tower Ops=Tower Control
[RADIO] Done: Tower Ops is called Tower Control now.
```

`+radio/modadd` and `+radio/modrem <frequency>=<player>` change who moderates it, and `+radio/admadd`
and `+radio/admrem` who runs it. A moderator may change the members list but not these:

```text
> +radio/modadd Harbour Watch=Eli
[RADIO] Done: Eli is one of the moderators of Harbour Watch now.
```

`+radio/delete <frequency>` asks first. Typing the name again after `=` deletes it, with everything
said on it and everyone's settings there:

```text
> +radio/delete Pier Gossip
[RADIO] Warning: This deletes Pier Gossip with everything said on it. Type +radio/delete Pier Gossip=Pier Gossip to do it.
> +radio/delete Pier Gossip=Pier Gossip
[RADIO] Done: Deleted Pier Gossip.
```

### Locks

Each frequency has three [locks](/reference/sharpmush-help/sharplock/#lock-bit):

| Lock | Decides who else may | Unless set |
|---|---|---|
| `talk` | tune in, recall and speak | everyone |
| `moderate` | moderate it | the wizard role and radio admins |
| `admin` | run it | the wizard role and radio admins |

Being on a list always passes its lock: a member passes `talk`, a moderator `moderate`, an admin
`admin`. `+radio/lock <frequency>/<lock>=<lock>` sets one, and `+radio/unlock <frequency>/<lock>`
puts back the default. Any lock works, such as `role^harbourmaster` for holders of a role, or
`#false` for nobody but the lists.

To close Tower Ops to everyone but its members:

```text
> +radio/lock Tower Ops/talk=#false
[RADIO] Done: The talk lock of Tower Ops is #false now.
> +radio/memadd Tower Ops=Eli
[RADIO] Done: Eli is one of the members of Tower Ops now.
```

Admins see the locks at the foot of `+radio/examine`, with how many lines it keeps:

```text
> +radio/examine Tower Ops
═════════════════════════════════╡ Tower Ops ╞════════════════════════════════
Category:   Port
About:      none
You:        not tuned in
Title:      none
Alterego:   none
Listening:  Eli
Admins:     Dana
Moderators: none
Members:    Eli
Talk lock:     #false
Moderate lock: role^wizard|perm^softcode.radio.admin
Admin lock:    role^wizard|perm^softcode.radio.admin
Keeps:         500 lines
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

A frequency closed to someone is left out of their `+radio` list.

### What a frequency keeps

`+radio/options <frequency>/<option>=<value>` sets what a frequency keeps. Leave the part after `=`
empty to put back the default.

| Option | Sets | Unless set |
|---|---|---|
| `keep` | how many lines it keeps for recall | 500 |
| `age` | drop lines older than this, such as `30d` | the server's default |
| `length` | the longest message, in characters | the server's default |

```text
> +radio/options Harbour Watch/keep=200
[RADIO] Done: Harbour Watch: keep is 200.
> +radio/options Harbour Watch/length=20
[RADIO] Done: Harbour Watch: length is 20.
```

A longer message is refused:

```text
> +radio Harbour=This message is far too long for the limit.
[RADIO] Warning: Harbour Watch takes messages of up to 20 characters.
```

## Setting up the radio

### Installing

Radio ships with the server but is not installed on a new game. An administrator installs it from
the portal: open **Packages** in the admin area, choose **Browse**, and install `radio`. Its `+help` topics and `+config` settings come with it, through the `radio-help` and `radio-config` bridges, wherever `+help` and `+config` are installed. See [Softcode Packages](/guides/packages/) for how
installing, upgrading and rolling back work.

It adds the **Radio** object to the master room and the command `+radio` for everyone. `+radio` is
a game command, matched before softcode `$`-commands, so a `+radio` your game already has in
softcode stops running once the package is in. A new game has no frequencies: create the first ones
as staff with `+radio/create`.

The `radio-scene` package, also in the catalogue and not installed on a new game, adds
`+radio/log`, which puts what a player hears on a frequency into the scene they are in, as
[radio poses](/guides/scene-pose-types/).

### Who runs the radio

The package adds the **Radio Admin** role (`radio-admin`), in a category of its own called Radio. A
radio admin creates frequencies, and moderates and runs every one of them, as the wizard role does.
Give it with `@role/assign`:

```text
> @role/assign *Eli=radio-admin
Eli now holds radio-admin.
> +radio/create Pier Gossip=Social
[RADIO] Done: Created Pier Gossip. +radio/join Pier Gossip tunes in.
```

To let one player do the same without the role, allow them the permission instead:
`@permission/allow *Eli=softcode.radio.admin`. To run only one frequency, make them its admin with
`+radio/admadd`; to keep order on one, its moderator with `+radio/modadd`.

### Settings

Radio has no `+config` settings. Each frequency's limits are its [options](#what-a-frequency-keeps),
and who runs it is its lists and [locks](#locks). A few game-wide defaults are attributes on the
Radio object, changed with `&`:

| Attribute | What it is | Default |
|---|---|---|
| ``DATA`RECALL`` | How many lines `+radio/recall` shows when no number is given. | 50 |
| ``DATA`RECALL`MAX`` | The most lines one recall shows. | 500 |
| ``DATA`COLOR`` | The colour of a frequency's name for a listener who chose none. | `info` |
| ``DATA`LOCK`MODERATE`` | The moderate lock of a frequency that sets none. | `role^wizard\|perm^softcode.radio.admin` |
| ``DATA`LOCK`ADMIN`` | The admin lock of a frequency that sets none, and who may create frequencies. | `role^wizard\|perm^softcode.radio.admin` |

<Aside>
Each frequency is a feed, `radio/<number>`, which the server keeps with its lines and its
listeners; `help @feed` in the game covers feeds. Softcode can read a frequency's lines with
`feedrecall()`, and another package can act on every line with a
[feed tap](/guides/extension-points/#points-the-server-offers), the way `radio-scene` does.
</Aside>
