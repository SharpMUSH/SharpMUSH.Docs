The **duties** package adds `+duty`: short adventures a party plays through together. The
party goes from challenge to challenge. At each one everyone rolls, and how the party did
picks the way on. A duty can hand out rewards on the way and at its end.

- Every player can write a duty. A duty reviewer approves it, or sends it back with a note.
- Staff choose which approved duties players can start, and on which days.
- Everyone always rolls, and everyone always has something to roll.
- Steps nobody in the party has seen are taken first, so a duty played again goes somewhere new.
- There are no timers. The party's leader keeps things moving.

| A player | write a duty | [Writing a duty](#writing-a-duty) |

In the game, `+help duty` has the same in short form.

## Playing

`+duty` lists the duties you can start now, with your progress in each: the rewards you
have received and the challenges you have seen. Under them are the parties gathering.

`+duty <duty>` shows one duty: its description, the party it wants, and your progress.

To play:

1. `+duty/start <duty>` gathers a party, and tells you its number.
2. Others join with `+duty/join <number>`.
3. The leader sets out with `+duty/go` once enough are there.

At a challenge, `+duty` shows the options:

```
+=======================< The Harbor Fire - The Gate >=======================+
| A burning gate.                                                            |
|                                                                            |
| Option     Roll                                Trait    Difficulty  Needs  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| force      Winning                             Winning  standard    1 of 1 |
| sneak      Skill (trained)                     Skill    hard        1 of 1 |
| improvise  Improvise                           Winning  extreme     1 roll |
+============================================================================+
| Round 1. Waiting on Ana and Ben. 2 ways lead on. +duty/try <option> to     |
| roll.                                                                      |
+============================================================================+
```

Everyone rolls once each round with `+duty/try <option>`:

- **Needs** is how many more successes the option needs. When an option still needs more
  after everyone has rolled, everyone rolls another round.
- An option marked `trained` can be rolled only by someone trained in its trait.
- `improvise` is always there. It is one difficulty harder than the hardest option.

When everyone has rolled and no option is still waiting, the challenge has its outcome.
The party's result is what most of its members got. Members who succeeded receive the
challenge's rewards. Then the party goes on to the next step.

At a narration, the leader goes on with `+duty/go`. At an end, the duty is over: a
successful end counts as finishing it.

### Keeping the party moving

| Command | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| `+duty/leave` | anyone | Leave the party |
| `+duty/force <name>` | the leader | Roll Improvise for someone who has not rolled |
| `+duty/kick <name>` | the leader | Send someone away. Their roll this round no longer counts |
| `+duty/abandon` | the leader | End the duty. Nobody earns its rewards |

When the leader leaves, whoever has been in the party longest leads. When the last member
leaves, the duty ends.

`+duty/info <duty>` shows what you have learned from a duty.

## Writing a duty

A duty is a set of **steps** joined by **links**. Each step is one of:

- a **challenge**: everyone rolls, and the result picks the link to follow;
- a **narration**: text to read, then the leader goes on;
- an **end**: the duty is over, in success or failure.

Start one, give it a description, and add its steps. The first step is where it starts.

```
> +duty/create harbor=The Harbor Fire
[DUTY] Done: Created harbor. Add its first step with +duty/node harbor/<step>=challenge.
> +duty/set harbor/description=Smoke over the docks.
> +duty/node harbor/gate=challenge
[DUTY] Done: gate is a challenge. harbor starts there.
```

`+duty/set <duty>/<field>=<value>` changes the duty's `title`, `description`, `start`,
`repeat` (`once` or `every`), `min` and `max` (the party's size). With a step's name in the
middle, it changes the step: its `label`, its `text`, and `seen`, the text for someone who
has been there before. A challenge also takes an outcome text for each result:
`crit-success`, `success`, `partial`, `fail` and `crit-fail`. An end takes `result`,
`success` or `fail`.

### Options

A challenge's options are what the party can roll:

```
> +duty/option harbor/gate/force=win standard
[DUTY] Done: gate/force rolls win at standard.
```

After the trait and the difficulty come, if you want them, the successes needed and the
failures allowed, then `trained`: `+duty/option harbor/gate/climb=athletics hard 2 1 trained`.
The difficulty is `trivial`, `easy`, `standard`, `hard` or `extreme`.

Every challenge needs one option anyone can roll: an untrained option on a trait the sheet
system lets everyone use.

### Links

`+duty/link <duty>/<from>/<port>=<to>` joins two steps:

```
> +duty/link harbor/gate/success=hall
[DUTY] Done: Link 1: gate on success leads to hall.
```

- A challenge leaves by `success` and `fail`. It can also have links on `crit-success`,
  `partial` and `crit-fail`; without them those results go by success or fail.
- A narration leaves by `continue`.
- After the step, a link can take settings: `weight=<n>`, `min=<n>` and `max=<n>` (how many
  members succeeded), `share=<percent>` of the party, and `requires=<option>` (an option
  that must have succeeded). It is used only when all of them hold.
- Several links on one port are a choice. The steps the fewest members have seen come first,
  then the weights decide.
- Every challenge needs a link on success and one on fail with no settings, so the party
  always has a way on.

### Rewards

`+duty/reward <duty>/<step>=<kind> [once|every] <details>` adds a reward to any step:

```
> +duty/reward harbor/gate=coin once 5
[DUTY] Done: Reward 1 on gate: coin, once.
```

- `info` is something learned. Its details are the text, which `+duty/info` shows again.
- The sheet system adds its own kinds, such as coin. Its details are a JSON object of the
  kind's fields, or the value of its first field.
- `once` gives a reward to each character only the first time. It is the default.
- A challenge gives its rewards to the members who succeeded. A narration or an end gives
  them to everyone there.

### Checking and submitting

`+duty/steps <duty>` shows every step, option, link and reward, with the links numbered.
`+duty/check <duty>` lists what still needs work:

```
> +duty/check harbor
+============================< harbor needs work >===========================+
| Nothing leads to hall.                                                     |
| Nothing leads to loot.                                                     |
| Nothing leads to lost.                                                     |
| gate needs a link on success with no conditions.                           |
| gate needs a link on fail with no conditions.                              |
+============================================================================+
```

When nothing is left, `+duty/submit <duty>` sends it to the duty reviewers.
`+duty/withdraw <duty>` takes it back to change it. `+duty/mine` lists your duties.

Changing a duty after it is approved makes a new draft beside it. Players keep playing the
approved one until the draft is approved too. `+duty/discard <duty>` drops the draft.

## Reviewing

Duty reviewers hold `softcode.duties.review`, which the **Duty Reviewer** role gives:

```
@role/assign *Ann=duty-reviewer
```

- `+duty/review` lists the duties waiting; `+duty/review <duty>` shows one with every step.
- `+duty/approve <duty>` approves it.
- `+duty/return <duty>=<note>` sends it back to its author with the note.
- `+duty/note <duty>/<step>=<note>` leaves a note on one step.
- `+duty/retire <duty>` takes an approved duty out of use, and `+duty/restore` brings it back.
- `+duty/end <number>` ends any party's duty.

## The active roster

Approving a duty does not make it playable. Players can start a duty only while it is on
the **active roster**:

```
> +duty/roster harbor=on
[DUTY] Done: harbor is on the active roster.
```

`+duty/roster` on its own lists the approved duties, whether each is on the roster, and
its window.

A window limits the days a duty can be started: `+duty/window harbor=2026-12-01,2026-12-31`.
Add `,yearly` for days of every year, written `MM-DD`. A yearly window may wrap the new
year: `+duty/window frost=12-01,01-06,yearly`. `+duty/window harbor=` clears it.

The roster is also a table in `+config duties`, beside the sheet system to use, the most
steps one duty may have, and the largest party.

## Installing

Install **Duties** from the portal's package list, or with the package manager. Install
**Duties help** and **Duties settings** too, for `+help duty` and `+config duties`.

<Aside type="caution">
Duties roll nothing on their own. Install a sheet system bridge as well, such as
`duties-d20` or `duties-fate`. Without one, duties can be written but not checked or played.
</Aside>

## Sheet systems

A sheet system bridge attaches `` PROVIDER`<NAME> `` to the Duties object, holding its own
object. With several installed, `+config duties/system` picks one by name. The object answers:

| Attribute | Gets | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| `` FN`DUTIES`TRAITS `` | | A JSON array of `{"key","label","group","always"}`. `always` marks traits everyone can roll |
| `` FN`DUTIES`CHECK `` | `%<roller>`, `%<trait>`, `%<difficulty>`, `%<mods>`, `%<context>` | `{"tier","text"}`, the tier one of `crit-fail`, `fail`, `partial`, `success`, `crit-success` |
| `` FN`DUTIES`REWARDS `` | | A JSON array of `{"kind","label","fields":[{"key","type"}]}` |
| `` ON`DUTIES`GRANT `` | `%<roller>`, `%<reward>`, `%<context>` | Triggered to hand a reward out. `%<reward>` has `kind`, `repeat` and the kind's fields |
| `` FN`DUTIES`TRAINED `` | `%<roller>`, `%<trait>` | Optional. 1 when they are trained in it |
| `` FN`DUTIES`CAN `` | `%<roller>`, `%<trait>` | Optional. 1 when they may roll it |
| `` FN`DUTIES`FALLBACK `` | `%<roller>`, `%<context>` | Optional. The trait Improvise rolls |

Duties keeps track of `once` rewards itself, so a system only hands out what it is given.
