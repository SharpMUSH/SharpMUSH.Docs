The web portal's live scene page works on a phone as well as on a desktop. This page covers:

- how a live scene looks on a phone;
- how an unsent pose follows you from one device to another;
- adding the scene schedule to a calendar app.

All of it comes with the Scene plugin. On a game without it, the portal shows no Scenes section at all.

## A live scene on a phone

Open a running scene from **Scenes**, then **Live now**. On a screen narrower than a tablet, each pose
is a card: the portrait, name and time sit above the text, so the text uses the whole width.
Out-of-character lines keep their own colour band.

The box you write in is a sheet at the bottom of the screen. The IC and OOC buttons and the format
menu sit in a row along its top, and the field sits beside a round Send button.

![A live scene on a phone: each pose is a card with the name above the full-width text, and the composer is a sheet with IC and OOC along the top and a round Send button beside the field.](../../../assets/guide/scene-phone-draft.webp)

### Typing with the on-screen keyboard

When the keyboard opens, the page shrinks to the part of the screen the keyboard leaves. The
scene's name stays at the top, the poses scroll in the middle, and the compose sheet sits directly
on the keyboard. Nothing jumps when the keyboard opens or closes, so you do not lose your place in
the scene.

![The live scene page on a phone with the keyboard open (simulated in this picture): the scene's title in one line at the top, the last pose, and the compose sheet resting on the keyboard.](../../../assets/guide/scene-phone-keyboard.webp)

## Your draft follows you

What you type into a live scene is saved as you type, both in the browser and on the server. Open
the same scene on another device, as the same character, and the field holds the same text. You can
start a pose on your phone on the bus and finish it at your desk.

The icon beside the format menu shows where the draft is:

| Icon | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Cloud with a tick | Saved. Your other devices open this draft. |
| Cloud with an arrow | Saving. You typed in the last moment. |
| Cloud crossed out | Kept on this device only. The server could not be reached; the draft is sent when it can be. |

A screen reader announces the same words: "Draft saved", "Saving draft" and "Draft on this device
only".

When you send the pose, the draft empties on every device. A device that was open in the
background empties the next time you switch back to it.

![The phone after the pose was sent from the desktop: the new pose is the last card in the scene, and the field is empty again.](../../../assets/guide/scene-phone-sent.webp)

![The same scene on a desktop browser, opened after typing on the phone: the field holds the pose that was started on the phone.](../../../assets/guide/scene-desktop-draft.webp)

Each character has its own draft for each scene. A guest, or a character who cannot see the scene,
keeps the draft in the browser only.

<Aside>
If two devices type at the same moment, the one that saved last wins. Switch back to a device and
it shows the newer draft.
</Aside>

## Scenes in your calendar

The **Scheduled** list under **Scenes** has an **Add to calendar** button. It opens a card with
calendar addresses. Paste an address into a calendar app (Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook
and Thunderbird all accept one), or press **Subscribe** to hand it to the calendar app on your
device.

Each scene appears at your own local time. The feed gives every start time in UTC, and the
calendar app converts it to the zone your device is set to, so two players in different countries
both see the right time. Scenes have no end time, so each event lasts two hours. The calendar app
checks for changes every hour.

![The Add to calendar card on a phone: the public address with Copy and Subscribe buttons, and a private link section that says when the link was made, with Make a new link and Turn it off buttons.](../../../assets/guide/scene-calendar-phone.webp)

![The private link section on a phone just after the link was made: a checkbox for Only scenes I run or joined, the address with Copy and Subscribe, and a note that the address is shown only now.](../../../assets/guide/scene-calendar-phone-link.webp)

![The Add to calendar card on a desktop browser, above the scheduled scenes: the public address and a private link, each with Copy and Subscribe.](../../../assets/guide/scene-calendar-desktop.webp)

### The public feed

The first address lists every public scene on the schedule. Anyone can use it, without signing in,
so a game can also put it on its website.

```text
https://<your game>/api/scenes/calendar.ics
```

It lists the scenes that were given a start time: those still to come, those running, and those that started in
the last 14 days. A scene started on the spot, with no time set, does not appear.

### Your private link

A private link adds the private scenes your character can see. Press **Make my private link**, then
copy the address before you leave the page: the server keeps only a fingerprint of it, so it cannot
show it to you again.

Tick **Only scenes I run or joined** before you copy it to leave out the scenes you have nothing to
do with. That adds `?scope=mine` to the address.

Anyone who has the address can read the feed, so keep it to yourself. If it gets out, press **Make
a new link**: the old address stops working at once. **Turn it off** removes the link without
making a new one.

The link belongs to the character you are playing in that browser tab. Switch characters to make a
link for another one.

### What an event holds

This is a scene from a test server's public feed, as a calendar app reads it:

```text
BEGIN:VEVENT
UID:scene-2@localhost
DTSTAMP:20261015T065628Z
LAST-MODIFIED:20261015T061529Z
DTSTART:20261017T061525Z
DTEND:20261017T081525Z
SUMMARY:The Northern Road
DESCRIPTION:The caravan leaves at dawn\; who rides with it?\n\nRun by God\n
 \nhttps://localhost:8081/scenes/2
URL:https://localhost:8081/scenes/2
STATUS:CONFIRMED
TRANSP:TRANSPARENT
END:VEVENT
```

The title is the scene's title. The description holds its pitch, who runs it, and a link to the
scene in the portal. A scene that has a room also gives the room's name as the event's location.
The event is marked as free time, so it does not block your calendar.
