**Jobs** are how players ask staff for something and how staff keep track of it: a room fix, a
bug, a plot idea, an application. Each request is a job with a running conversation between the
player and staff. Each job sits in a **bucket**, such as Requests, Building or Bugs, and the bucket
decides who works it.

<SharpieIllustration scene="jobs" />

A job is always waiting on someone. It is **New** until staff pick it up, then **With staff** or
**Waiting on you** as the two sides reply, **On hold** when staff park it, and **Closed** once it is
approved, denied, completed or canceled.

| You are | You want to | Read |
|---|---|---|
| A player | ask staff for something | [Sending a request](#sending-a-request) |
| A player | read staff's answer and reply | [Following your jobs](#following-your-jobs) |
| A player | let a friend follow a job with you | [Helpers](#helpers) |
| Staff | see what is waiting | [The queue](#the-queue) |
| Staff | take a job and answer it | [Working a job](#working-a-job) |
| Staff | close a job | [Closing a job](#closing-a-job) |
| Staff | change what a bucket does, or who works it | [Buckets](#buckets) |
| Staff | install it | [Running Jobs](#running-jobs) |

In the game, `+help jobs` has the player commands in short form, `+help jobs staff` the staff ones,
`+help jobs commands` every switch, and `+help buckets` the bucket settings. `+job`, `+jobs`,
`+myjob`, `+myjobs`, `+ticket` and `+tickets` are all the same command, so use the one you know.
A switch can be cut short to any start only it has: `/rep` is `/reply`.

Buckets can also answer, approve and track jobs on their own: first replies, measured waits,
checklists, status updates and approving at once are in [Jobs That Run Themselves](/guides/jobs-automation/).

## Sending a request

`+buckets` lists the buckets you can file in, with how soon each usually answers:

```text
> +buckets
════════════════════════════════╡ Job buckets ╞═══════════════════════════════
Bucket        Open  First answer   What it is for
Applications     0  within 3 days  Character applications.
Bugs             0  within 7 days  Problems with code or the server.
Building         0  within 7 days  Rooms, objects, descriptions and typos.
Feedback         0  within 7 days  Suggestions and complaints. Staff do not
                                   see who sent them.
Plots            0  within 7 days  Plot and scene proposals.
Requests         0  within 3 days  Anything you need from staff.
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
+request [<bucket>/]<title>=<text>
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

`+request <title>=<text>` files a job in Requests:

```text
> +request Lantern for my shop=Could my shop on Cooper's Row get a lit lantern over the door? It's too dark to find at night.
[JOBS] Done: Filed job 1 in Requests: Lantern for my shop. Staff aim to answer within 3 days. +job 1 shows it.
```

To file in another bucket, put its full name and a `/` before the title:

```text
> +request Building/Shop sign typo=The sign over our shop reads "Kettel & Co". It should be "Kettle & Co".
[JOBS] Done: Filed job 3 in Building: Shop sign typo. Staff aim to answer within 7 days. +job 3 shows it.
```

The part before the `/` counts as a bucket only when it is a bucket's whole name, so
`+request Help w/ exits=...` is just a title in Requests.

Four shortcuts file straight into one bucket: `+bug` into Bugs, `+typo` into Building, `+pitch`
into Plots and `+apply` into Applications.

```text
> +bug The ferry exit=Typing ferry from the Quay says "I don't see that here." even though the exit shows in the room.
[JOBS] Done: Filed job 2 in Bugs: The ferry exit. Staff aim to answer within 7 days. +job 2 shows it.
```

Messages are markdown, so `**bold**` and lists work. Nothing you write is run as code, so brackets
and percent signs are safe. To show text exactly as typed, such as a piece of code, add `/softcode`:
`+bug/softcode <title>=<text>`. `+help jobs format` has the rest.

## Following your jobs

`+jobs` lists your open jobs. A `*` marks one with something you have not read:

```text
> +jobs
═══════════════════════════════════╡ Jobs ╞═══════════════════════════════════
 #  Bucket    Title                                    Status          Updated
 2  Bugs      The ferry exit                           New             Oct 16
*1  Requests  Lantern for my shop                      Waiting on you  Oct 16
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
+job <#> reads one · +job/old lists your closed ones
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

You are told when staff answer, and get a copy by @mail:

```text
[JOBS] Ash replied to your job 1: Lantern for my shop. +job 1 shows it.
MAIL: You have a new message (1) from Jobs.
```

`+job <#>` reads a job, every message under a line saying who wrote it and when. Message 2 is
the bucket's own first reply, which [Jobs That Run Themselves](/guides/jobs-automation/#first-replies)
explains:

```text
> +job 1
════════════════════════╡ Job 1: Lantern for my shop ╞════════════════════════
Bucket:   Requests
Status:   Waiting on you
Priority: Normal
Filed:    Oct 16 00:51 by Bea
Handlers: Ash
══════════════════════════╡ Bea · Oct 16 00:51 · #1 ╞═════════════════════════
Could my shop on Cooper's Row get a lit lantern over the door? It's too dark
to find at night.
═══════════════════════╡ Requests · Oct 16 00:51 · #2 ╞═══════════════════════
Thanks. Staff have your request and will answer here.

Staff aim to answer within 3 days.
══════════════════════════╡ Ash · Oct 16 00:51 · #3 ╞═════════════════════════
Happy to. Do you want a plain iron lantern, or one with the shop's sign
painted on the glass?
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
+job/reply 1=<text> answers
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

`+job/reply <#>=<text>` answers, and hands the turn back to staff:

```text
> +job/reply 1=The painted one, please: a copper kettle on blue glass. Mira keeps the shop with me.
[JOBS] Done: You replied to job 1.
```

When staff close a job you are told how, and the job moves to `+jobs/old`:

```text
[JOBS] Ash approved your job 1: Lantern for my shop. +job 1 shows it.
> +jobs/old
════════════════════════════════╡ Closed jobs ╞═══════════════════════════════
 #  Bucket    Title                                          Status    Updated
*1  Requests  Lantern for my shop                            Approved  Oct 16
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
+job <#> reads one
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

A reply within a few days of a job closing opens it again, so a follow-up stays with the rest of
the conversation. How many days is the bucket's `reopen` setting; Requests allows 7:

```text
> +job/reply 1=One more thing: could it light itself at dusk?
[JOBS] Done: You replied to job 1 and opened it again.
```

Past that, file a new request and mention the old job's number.

A few more things you can do with your own jobs:

- `+job/cancel <#>` withdraws a job you filed.
- `+jobs/catchup` marks everything read.
- `+job/options mail=off` stops the @mail copies; `=on` brings them back.

```text
> +job/cancel 2
[JOBS] Done: Job 2 is closed: canceled.
> +job/options mail=off
[JOBS] Done: Job mail is off.
```

### Helpers

`+job/addhelper <#>=<player>` lets someone else read a job and reply to it, as you can.
`+job/remhelper <#>=<player>` takes that back, and a helper can remove themselves.

```text
> +job/addhelper 1=Mira
[JOBS] Done: Mira can read and answer job 1 now.
```

The job then shows in Mira's `+jobs`, and she hears when it closes:

```text
[JOBS] Ash approved job 1: Lantern for my shop. +job 1 shows it.
```

A helper never sees staff notes, the same as the player who filed the job.

## The queue

Staff are whoever works a bucket, and that comes from a role, not from being a wizard. Without one,
a wizard sees what a player sees. The **Job Admin** role (`job-admin`) works every bucket, and
each bucket has a role of its own, such as `job-admin-building`:

```text
> @role/assign me=job-admin
Ash now holds job-admin.
```

`+buckets` then marks every bucket you work with `*`, and shows Staff, a bucket hidden from players:

```text
> +buckets
════════════════════════════════╡ Job buckets ╞═══════════════════════════════
Bucket         Open  First answer   What it is for
Applications*     0  within 3 days  Character applications.
Bugs*             0  within 7 days  Problems with code or the server.
Building*         0  within 7 days  Rooms, objects, descriptions and typos.
Feedback*         0  within 7 days  Suggestions and complaints. Staff do not
                                    see who sent them.
Plots*            0  within 7 days  Plot and scene proposals.
Requests*         0  within 3 days  Anything you need from staff.
Staff*            0  within 7 days  Work among staff. Players do not see it.
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
* you work it · +request [<bucket>/]<title>=<text>
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

Staff who are connected hear every new job, and every reply from a player, in a bucket they work:

```text
[JOBS] Bea filed job 1 (Requests): Lantern for my shop
[JOBS] Bea replied to job 1 (Requests): Lantern for my shop
```

For staff, `+jobs` opens on what needs staff in the buckets they work, high priority first and
then whoever has waited longest, followed by their own open jobs:

```text
> +jobs
═══════════════════════════════════╡ Jobs ╞═══════════════════════════════════
 #  Bucket    Title                                            Status  Updated
*2  Bugs      The ferry exit                                   New     Oct 16
*1  Requests  Lantern for my shop                              New     Oct 16
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
+job <#> reads one · +jobs/all lists every open job
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

Other views:

| Type | To see |
|---|---|
| `+jobs <bucket>` | one bucket's open jobs |
| `+jobs/all` | every open job, including those waiting on a player |
| `+jobs/new`, `/mine`, `/overdue`, `/player`, `/held` | new jobs, the ones you handle, overdue ones, those waiting on a player, those on hold |
| `+jobs/from <player>` | jobs a player filed |
| `+jobs/who <player>` | jobs someone handles |
| `+job/old [<bucket>]` | closed jobs |
| `+job/next`, `+job/scan` | read the next unread job, or list them |

```text
> +jobs/all
═══════════════════════════════╡ All open jobs ╞══════════════════════════════
 #  Bucket    Title                                       Status       Updated
 2  Bugs      The ferry exit                              With player  Oct 16
*3  Building  Shop sign typo                              New          Oct 16
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
> +jobs Bugs
═════════════════════════════╡ Open jobs in Bugs ╞════════════════════════════
#  Bucket  Title                                          Status       Updated
2  Bugs    The ferry exit                                 With player  Oct 16
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
+job/old Bugs lists its closed jobs
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

A list longer than your screen comes in pages, and the page number goes last: `+jobs/2`,
`+jobs/new/2`, `+job/old/3 Requests`.

## Working a job

`+job/claim <#>` makes you the job's handler; `+job/unclaim` lets it go.
`+job/assign <#>=<names>` sets the handlers instead, and `+<role>` there hands a job to everyone
holding a role. `+job/watch <#>` tells you of every change without making you a handler.

```text
> +job/claim 1
[JOBS] Done: You handle job 1 now.
> +job/assign 2=Ash
[JOBS] Job 2 is handled by Ash.
```

`+job/reply <#>=<text>` answers the player, and makes it their turn. `+job/note <#>=<text>` writes a
note only staff see. It passes no turn and the player is not told:

```text
> +job/note 1=Lanterns on Cooper's Row are the lamplighters' plot. Checking with Tomas before I promise anything.
[JOBS] Done: You added a staff note to job 1.
> +job/reply 1=Happy to. Do you want a plain iron lantern, or one with the shop's sign painted on the glass?
[JOBS] Done: You replied to job 1.
```

Staff reading the job see the note, marked as staff only, and the due date. Bea's own copy of the
same job, [above](#following-your-jobs), has neither:

```text
> +job 1
════════════════════════╡ Job 1: Lantern for my shop ╞════════════════════════
Bucket:   Requests
Status:   Needs staff
Priority: Normal
Filed:    Oct 16 00:51 by Bea
Due:      Oct 19 00:51
Handlers: Ash
Helpers:  Mira
══════════════════════════╡ Bea · Oct 16 00:51 · #1 ╞═════════════════════════
Could my shop on Cooper's Row get a lit lantern over the door? It's too dark
to find at night.
═══════════════════════╡ Requests · Oct 16 00:51 · #2 ╞═══════════════════════
Thanks. Staff have your request and will answer here.

Staff aim to answer within 3 days.
═══════════════════╡ Ash (staff only) · Oct 16 00:51 · #3 ╞═══════════════════
Lanterns on Cooper's Row are the lamplighters' plot. Checking with Tomas
before I promise anything.
══════════════════════════╡ Ash · Oct 16 00:51 · #4 ╞═════════════════════════
Happy to. Do you want a plain iron lantern, or one with the shop's sign
painted on the glass?
══════════════════════════╡ Bea · Oct 16 00:52 · #5 ╞═════════════════════════
The painted one, please: a copper kettle on blue glass. Mira keeps the shop
with me.
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
+job/reply 1=<text> answers
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

`+job/all <#>` adds the job's log: who claimed it, moved it, changed its priority.

### Priority, due dates and holding

A new job is due when its bucket's `turnaround` runs out. It is overdue only while staff owe the
answer, never while it waits on the player.

```text
> +job/priority 2=high
[JOBS] Done: Job 2 is high priority.
> +job/due 2=+1d
[JOBS] Done: Job 2 is due Oct 17 00:53.
> +job/hold 1
[JOBS] Done: Job 1 is on hold.
```

`+job/due` takes `+12h`, `+3d`, `+1w`, a date, or `none`. Priority is `low`, `normal` or `high`.
`+job/state <#>=staff`, `player` or `held` sets whose turn it is by hand, and
`+job/trans <#>=<bucket>` moves a job to another bucket.

### Asking a player something

`+job/query <player>/<title>=<text>` opens a job for a player, already waiting on them. It is filed
in Requests:

```text
> +job/query Bea/Lantern at dusk=A self-lighting lantern needs the lamplighters' say. Can you wait until Friday's plot scene?
[JOBS] Done: Opened job 5 for Bea: Lantern at dusk
```

Bea hears `[JOBS] Ash opened your job 5: Lantern at dusk. +job 5 shows it.`, and her `+jobs` shows it
as **Waiting on you**. `+job/create <bucket>/<title>=<text>` opens a job of your own instead.

## Closing a job

`+job/approve`, `+job/deny`, `+job/complete` and `+job/cancel <#>=<text>` close a job. The text is
the last message, for the player to read; leave it out and the outcome is all they get.

```text
> +job/approve 1=Done: the lantern hangs over the door, kettle and all. Mira can light it too.
[JOBS] Done: Job 1 is closed: approved.
> +job/complete 3=Fixed the sign. Thanks for spotting it.
[JOBS] Done: Job 3 is closed: completed.
```

`+job/reopen <#>` opens a closed job again, waiting on staff with a fresh due date:

```text
> +job/reopen 4
[JOBS] Done: Job 4 is open again and waiting on staff.
```

A Job Admin can remove a job for good with `+job/delete <#>`, or fold one into another with
`+job/merge <from>=<into>`.

## Buckets

The game starts with seven buckets: Applications, Requests (where `+request` goes), Building, Bugs,
Plots, Feedback and Staff. `+bucket <name>` shows what one does. Staff who work it also see its
permission, role, locks and hooks:

```text
> +bucket Requests
═════════════════════════════╡ Bucket: Requests ╞═════════════════════════════
About:           Anything you need from staff.
Turnaround:      3 days
First answer:    Not measured yet
Closed in:       Not measured yet
Reopen:          7 days
Anonymous:       none
Hidden:          no
Taking jobs:     yes
Priority:        normal
Keep:            forever
Acknowledge:     Thanks. Staff have your request and will answer here.
Approve at once: no
Checklist:       None
Permission:      softcode.jobs.requests
Role:            job-admin-requests
File lock:       #TRUE
Work lock:       PERM^softcode.jobs.admin|PERM^softcode.jobs.requests
Hooks:           none written
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
+bucket/set Requests/<setting>=<value>
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

### Settings

A Job Admin, or anyone who works the bucket, changes a setting with
`+bucket/set <bucket>/<setting>=<value>`:

```text
> +bucket/set Requests/turnaround=2
[JOBS] Requests turnaround is 2.
> +bucket/set Building/description=Rooms, objects, descriptions, signs and typos.
[JOBS] Building description is Rooms, objects, descriptions, signs and typos.
```

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| `description` | what `+buckets` says the bucket is for |
| `turnaround` | days staff have to answer a new job before it is overdue; `0` for no due date |
| `reopen` | days after closing in which the player's reply opens a job again; `0` for never |
| `anonymous` | `filer` hides who filed from staff; `staff` hides staff names from players; `none` |
| `hidden` | `yes` shows the bucket only to those who work it |
| `priority` | what new jobs start at |
| `keep` | days a closed job stays; empty keeps them forever |

`acknowledge`, `checklist` and `audit` are covered in
[Jobs That Run Themselves](/guides/jobs-automation/).

Feedback starts with `anonymous` set to `filer`, so staff read the message but not the name:

```text
[JOBS] Anonymous filed job 6 (Feedback): Too many night scenes
> +job 6
═══════════════════════╡ Job 6: Too many night scenes ╞═══════════════════════
Bucket:       Feedback
Status:       New
Priority:     Normal
Filed:        Oct 16 00:55 by Anonymous
First answer: Within 7 days
Due:          Oct 23 00:55
Handlers:     Nobody
═══════════════════════╡ Anonymous · Oct 16 00:55 · #1 ╞══════════════════════
Most plot scenes start after midnight my time. Could some run earlier?
...
```

A Job Admin can still see who filed one:

```text
> +job/source 6
[JOBS] Job 6 was filed by Bea (#20:1792111559428).
```

### Who works a bucket

Give someone one bucket with its role. Here Mira works Building and nothing else:

```text
> @role/assign *Mira=job-admin-building
Mira now holds job-admin-building.
```

She now hears of new Building jobs and can work them:

```text
[JOBS] Bea filed job 4 (Building): Cooper's Row
> +job/claim 4
[JOBS] Done: You handle job 4 now.
> +job/complete 4=Fixed: cobbles.
[JOBS] Done: Job 4 is closed: completed.
```

A role of your own can allow the same permission: `@role/allow helper=softcode.jobs.requests`.
What is actually checked is the bucket's work lock, which `+bucket/lock <bucket>/work=<lock>` can
change; `/file` sets who may file there. `+help jobs roles` has more.

### Making and removing buckets

A Job Admin makes a bucket with `+bucket/create <name>=<description>`. It comes with its own
permission and role:

```text
> +bucket/create Festivals=Midwinter fair stalls, contests and prizes.
[JOBS] Done: Bucket Festivals is ready. The role job-admin-festivals works it: @role/assign <player>=job-admin-festivals
> +bucket/delete Festivals
[JOBS] Done: Bucket Festivals is deleted, with its role and permission.
```

`+bucket/delete` takes only an empty bucket; `+bucket/delete <bucket>=<other bucket>` moves its jobs
there first. `+bucket/rename` renames one, and `+bucket/disable` stops new jobs arriving while the
ones there are worked as before. Requests can be neither disabled nor deleted.

A bucket can also run code when its jobs change, in `` ON`<EVENT> `` attributes such as
`` ON`APPROVE ``. `+help jobs hooks` lists them.

## Running Jobs

### Installing

Jobs ships with the server but is not installed on a new game. An administrator installs it
during [first-run setup](/guides/packages/), where it is in the Staff group, or later from the
portal:

1. Open **Packages** in the admin area and choose **Browse**.
2. Install `jobs`. It needs `plus-config`, `plus-help` and `http-handler`; a new game already has
   them.
3. Install `jobs-app` too for the portal's **Jobs** page.

The `jobs-app` package adds a **Jobs** page to the portal at `/apps/jobs`, under Support in the
sidebar. Players file and follow their requests there, and staff work the buckets they hold, with
the same rules as in the game. See [Applications](/guides/applications/) for how portal
applications work.

See [Softcode Packages](/guides/packages/) for how installing, upgrading and rolling back work.

### Roles

| Role | Works |
|---|---|
| Job Admin (`job-admin`) | every bucket; can also delete jobs and make, change and remove buckets |
| Job Admin - Applications (`job-admin-applications`), and one per bucket | that bucket's jobs and settings |

Nobody holds these until they are given, wizards included.

### Settings

Each bucket's settings are changed with `+bucket/set`, as [above](#settings). The package's one
game setting, `jobs/wait-sample`, is a [`+config`](/guides/config/) value and is described in
[Jobs That Run Themselves](/guides/jobs-automation/#how-soon-a-bucket-answers).
