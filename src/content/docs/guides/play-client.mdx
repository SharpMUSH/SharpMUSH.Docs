---
title: Several Characters at Once
description: The website's client connects all your characters in one tab, with a rail to switch between them.
---

The **client** is the website's **Play** page for more than one of your characters. To open it,
choose **Play several characters** at the end of **Play**'s sidebar (on a phone, at the end of
**Play**'s menu), or go to `/client` on your game's website. **Play** shows that row only when your
account has more than one character. The client connects every one of your characters, and the rail
on the left lists them. Choose one to bring it to the front; the others stay logged in.

Everything **Play** has works the same way here: the terminal, the room, channels, pages, alerts
and settings. They belong to the character in front.

## Your characters

1. Sign in and open the client.
2. The character you were playing comes to the front. Your other characters connect behind it, and
   each gets a portrait on the rail, in the order your account lists them.

A tab connects at most 8 characters. With more than 8, the rail shows the first 8 your account
lists, and the character you were playing if it is not one of them; whichever of those would make a
ninth stays disconnected until you log another out.

A guest sees only the character it is playing.

## Switching

Choose a portrait on the rail. That character comes to the front with its own terminal output,
channels, pages and the line you had started typing; one that is not connected connects first. The character you left stays connected and
keeps receiving everything the game sends it.

You can choose the next portrait straight away. If you choose one while a character is still coming
to the front, the rail brings it forward next; if you choose several, only the last one comes.

The website acts as the character in front, as it does on any page: the wiki, scenes and your
profile all use that character.

Each character's own [theme](/guides/portal-themes/) colours the Play card and the column beside it.
The rest of the page keeps the theme of the character the tab started with, so switching changes
only the part you play in.

## The rail

Each portrait shows:

- a ring around the character in front;
- a green dot when the character is connected, grey when it is not (its portrait is dimmed);
- a number for unread channel messages and pages, on a character that is not in front;
- a small dot when it has new terminal lines but no unread messages.

A screen reader reads each portrait as its name, whether it is connected, and what it has
unread. For example: `Brin · Connected · 2 unread`.

## Logging a character out

1. Switch to the character.
2. Choose the power button in the terminal.

The character quits the game. Its portrait stays on the rail, dimmed; choosing it again connects it.

## Reloading the page

A reload connects every character again. The one in front reconnects as **Play** always does; the
others reconnect behind it.

## On a phone

The rail moves into **Play**'s menu: open the menu at the top of the terminal, and the portraits
are at the top under **Your characters**.
