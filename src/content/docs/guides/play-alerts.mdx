An **alert** is a rule you set in the website's **Play** page. It looks for text in each new line
the game sends. It can colour or style the line, or only the words it found, play a sound, or
raise a notification while the tab is in the background. Under **Advanced**, a rule can also hide
a line or show other text in place of what it found. Most MU\* clients call these highlights or
triggers.

Your rules are kept on your account, so every browser you sign in on uses the same ones.

## Adding a rule

1. On **Play**, open the terminal settings (the sliders button) and choose **Alerts…**.
2. Choose **Add rule** and type what to look for, such as your character's name.
3. Pick a colour, a sound, **Notify**, or any mix of them.
4. Type or paste a line under **Try a line** to see what the rules would do to it.
5. Choose **Save**.

A rule changes the lines that arrive after you save it, not the ones already on screen.

Alerts need a signed-in account. A guest does not see **Alerts…**.

## What a rule looks for

**Look for** is read one of five ways, chosen under **Read as**:

| Read as | Matches | Example | Matches | Does not match |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whole words | the words as typed, not inside a longer word | `Ann` | `Ann waves.` | `Anne waves.` |
| Contains | the text as typed, anywhere, inside a longer word too | `ann` | `Joanna waves.` | `Bob waves.` |
| Starts with | the text as typed, at the start of the line | `<OOC>` | `<OOC> Ann: hi` | `Ann says <OOC>` |
| Wildcards | the whole line; `*` is any run of characters, `?` one, `\` makes the next one plain | `* pages: *` | `Ann pages: hi` | `Ann says, "pages: hi"` |
| Regular expression | a .NET regular expression found anywhere in the line | `\bOOC\b` | `<OOC> Ann says hi` | `Snooc` |

Letters match in either case unless **Match case** is ticked.

**In** narrows a rule to some lines:

- **Every line**.
- **Pages**: lines the game marks as a page.
- **Channels**: lines the game marks as a channel line.
- **Everything else**: the room, emits and the game's replies.

The game marks pages and channel lines itself, so these work whatever the game's channel format
looks like.

The commands you type are never matched.

When several rules match one line, the first rule in the list that colours the whole line sets
its colour, the first with a sound sets the sound, and the line notifies if any of them notifies.
Where two rules colour the same words, the earlier rule wins.

A rule can have at most 200 characters, and an account keeps at most 50 rules. A regular expression
that takes too long on a line counts as no match for that line.

## What a rule colours

**Apply to** says which part of the line a rule's colour, style and rewrite go on:

| Apply to | Colours | Example rule | On `Bob waves to Ann.` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whole line | the line | `Ann` | the whole line |
| Each match | every place the rule found, and nothing else | `Ann` | only `Ann` |
| One group | one part of what the rule found, named under **Group** | `* waves to *`, group `1` | only `Bob` |

Groups come from wildcards and regular expressions. A wildcard numbers each `*` and `?` from 1,
left to right, and a `*` takes as little of the line as it can, so in `* pages: *` group 1 is the
name before the first `pages:`. A regular expression numbers its `( )` groups from 1, and a named
group `(?<who>…)` can be given by name, as `who`. Group `0` is all of what the rule found. **Whole
words**, **Contains** and **Starts with** have only group `0`. A rule that names a group its
pattern does not have cannot be saved.

A few lines show something other than their text, such as a picture or a table the game draws.
**Each match** and **One group** leave those lines as they are; **Whole line** still colours them.

## Colours

The colours are the theme's own: **Accent**, **Second accent**, **Third accent**, **Info**,
**Success**, **Warning** and **Danger**. A coloured line gets a tint and a bar down its left edge,
so it still stands out for a reader who cannot tell the colours apart. It arrives a little
brighter and settles over two seconds. Coloured words get the tint behind them.

## Advanced

Each rule has an **Advanced** section, closed until you open it:

| Choice | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Colour as** | **Tint behind**, the default, or **Text colour**, which colours the letters instead. Text the game already coloured keeps its own colour inside a whole line. |
| **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Strike-through** | Styles added to what the rule applies to. They work with or without a colour. |
| **Show instead** | Text shown in place of what the rule applies to. `$1`, `$2` and so on put in a group, `${who}` a named group, `$0` all of what was found, and `$$` a dollar sign. |
| **Hide the line** | The line is not shown. It still plays its sound and notifies, and the log keeps it. |
| **Skip later rules** | Rules below this one are not tried on a line it matches. |

For example, a regular expression rule with **Look for** `<(\w+)>`, **Apply to** **Each match**
and **Show instead** `[$1]` shows `<OOC> Ann: hi` as `[OOC] Ann: hi`.

**Show instead** and **Hide the line** change only what the terminal shows. The kept log and an
exported log hold the line as the game sent it, and a line brought back from the log is shown the
way the rules show it.

A rule with **Skip later rules** and nothing else is a way to keep some lines away from the rules
after it: put it first, with what those lines look like.

## Sounds and notifications

There are three sounds: **Chime**, **Ping** and **Knock**. Choosing one plays it, and the play
button beside it plays it again. Many lines arriving together play one sound, not one each.

**Notify** shows a browser notification with the character's name and the line, but only while
the Play tab is not in front. Clicking it brings the tab back. The first time you tick it, the
browser asks whether the site may show notifications; allow it. If the browser blocks them, the
dialog says so: allow notifications for the site in the browser's settings, or use a sound.

<Aside>
These notifications come from the open Play tab. To be told of a page or new mail when no tab is
open, use the game's [Notifications](/guides/notifications) instead.
</Aside>

## After a reload or a reconnect

The game numbers every line it sends you. When the page reloads or the connection drops, the
game sends again the lines you missed, and the page keeps the number of the last line that played
a sound or notified. A line that already alerted you does not alert again, and the screen a reload
brings back is coloured but stays quiet.
