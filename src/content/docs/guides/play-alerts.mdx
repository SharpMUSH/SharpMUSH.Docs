An **alert** is a rule you set in the website's **Play** page. It looks for words in each new line
the game sends. A line it matches can be coloured, play a sound, or raise a notification while
the tab is in the background. Most MU\* clients call these highlights or triggers.

Your rules are kept on your account, so every browser you sign in on uses the same ones.

## Adding a rule

1. On **Play**, open the terminal settings (the sliders button) and choose **Alerts…**.
2. Choose **Add rule** and type what to look for, such as your character's name.
3. Pick a colour, a sound, **Notify**, or any mix of them.
4. Type or paste a line under **Try a line** to see what the rules would do to it.
5. Choose **Save**.

A rule changes the lines that arrive after you save it, not the ones already on screen.

Alerts need a signed-in account. A guest does not see **Alerts…**.

## What a rule looks for

**Look for** is read one of three ways, chosen under **Read as**:

| Read as | Matches | Example | Matches | Does not match |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whole words | the words as typed, not inside a longer word | `Ann` | `Ann waves.` | `Anne waves.` |
| Wildcards | the whole line; `*` is any run of characters, `?` one, `\` makes the next one plain | `* pages: *` | `Ann pages: hi` | `Ann says, "pages: hi"` |
| Regular expression | a .NET regular expression found anywhere in the line | `\bOOC\b` | `<OOC> Ann says hi` | `Snooc` |

Letters match in either case unless **Match case** is ticked.

**In** narrows a rule to some lines:

- **Every line**.
- **Pages**: lines the game marks as a page.
- **Channels**: lines the game marks as a channel line.
- **Everything else**: the room, emits and the game's replies.

The game marks pages and channel lines itself, so these work whatever the game's channel format
looks like.

The commands you type are never matched.

When several rules match one line, the first rule in the list with a colour sets the colour, the
first with a sound sets the sound, and the line notifies if any of them notifies.

A rule can have at most 200 characters, and an account keeps at most 50 rules. A regular expression
that takes too long on a line counts as no match for that line.

## Colours

The colours are the theme's own: **Accent**, **Second accent**, **Third accent**, **Info**,
**Success**, **Warning** and **Danger**. A coloured line gets a tint and a bar down its left edge,
so it still stands out for a reader who cannot tell the colours apart. It arrives a little
brighter and settles over two seconds.

## Sounds and notifications

There are three sounds: **Chime**, **Ping** and **Knock**. Choosing one plays it, and the play
button beside it plays it again. Many lines arriving together play one sound, not one each.

**Notify** shows a browser notification with the character's name and the line, but only while
the Play tab is not in front. Clicking it brings the tab back. The first time you tick it, the
browser asks whether the site may show notifications; allow it. If the browser blocks them, the
dialog says so: allow notifications for the site in the browser's settings, or use a sound.

<Aside>
These notifications come from the open Play tab. To be told of a page or new mail when no tab is
open, use the game's [Notifications](/guides/notifications) instead.
</Aside>

## After a reload or a reconnect

The game numbers every line it sends you. When the page reloads or the connection drops, the
game sends again the lines you missed, and the page keeps the number of the last line that played
a sound or notified. A line that already alerted you does not alert again, and the screen a reload
brings back is coloured but stays quiet.
