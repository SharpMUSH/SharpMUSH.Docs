## What a MUSH is

A MUSH is a text game that many people play at once, in real time. The world is made of rooms joined
by exits. Each player has a character, and what they say and do is seen by the others in the same
room. Most MUSHes are about roleplay: players write their characters' actions and speech, and the
story comes from what they write together.

What sets a MUSH apart from roleplay in a forum or a chat server is that the world itself can be
programmed. Rooms, objects and commands are made in the game, by staff and often by players, in a
language called **softcode**. A game's character sheets, dice, bulletin boards and scene logs are
usually softcode that its staff wrote or installed.

## What SharpMUSH is

SharpMUSH is a server for running a MUSH. It runs PennMUSH softcode and imports PennMUSH, TinyMUX
and RhostMUSH databases, so an existing game can move to it and keep its code. Around that it adds:

- **Two ways in.** Players connect with a MU\* client over telnet, or play in the browser through the
  game's own website. Both reach the same world.
- **A website with the game.** Every server serves a [web portal](/guides/web-portal/) with a terminal,
  mail, scene logs, a wiki, character profiles and the staff's admin pages.
- **Accounts.** A player signs in once, with a password or with [Discord or Google](/guides/sign-in-services/),
  and plays any of the characters on their account.
- **Roles.** Staff powers are roles the game names and shapes, held by characters and accounts,
  in place of a single wizard flag.
- **Systems as packages.** Scenes, jobs, bulletin boards, character applications and the rest are
  [softcode packages](/guides/packages/) that staff install, upgrade and remove from the portal.
- **Pages from softcode.** A game builds its own [portal pages and forms](/guides/applications/) in
  softcode, and lays them out in the portal's App Builder.
